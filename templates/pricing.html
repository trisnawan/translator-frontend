Frequently asked questions

Can I change my plan later?

Yes, upgrade or downgrade anytime. Upgrades are prorated for the current cycle; downgrades apply at the start of your next billing cycle.

What payment methods do you accept?

All major credit cards, PayPal, and bank transfers for Enterprise plans. Every payment is processed securely through Stripe.

Is there a free trial for paid plans?

Yes! Professional comes with a 14-day free trial - no credit card required. Explore every feature before you decide.

What happens to my data if I cancel?

Your data is retained for 30 days after cancellation, so you can export it or reactivate. After that, it is permanently deleted.
